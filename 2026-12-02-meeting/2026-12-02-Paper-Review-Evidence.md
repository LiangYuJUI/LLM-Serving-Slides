## Unresolved checks

- Resolve the Figure 17 / Figure 19 numbering discrepancy against the versioned v1 PDF page before any future source renumbering.
- Verify the exact GPU model/count and any tensor/pipeline parallel settings for the emulated evaluation; the deck deliberately does not infer them from the Alibaba instance name.
- Audit whether LLMServingSim 2.0 can model SSD KV migration, restore timing, write/read contention, and required multi-tier cache behavior. Its documented storage idle-power setting alone does not prove those capabilities.
- Validate the proposed phase-by-operation energy models and storage / transfer energy against measurements before using them to claim lower energy or carbon.
- Search and reproduce adjacent work before treating the proposed multi-tier energy optimization as a confirmed research gap.
